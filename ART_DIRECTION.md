# Banana Escape Art Direction

Banana Escape should feel like a premium mobile runner with a playful cartoon finish: glossy, bold, readable at a glance, and expressive in motion.

## Visual Goal

- Target feel: premium runner polish, bright arcade readability, toy-like materials
- Core promise: "cute banana hero escaping absurd smoothie danger"
- Audience: broad casual mobile players, kid-safe, social-video friendly
- Art benchmark: high readability and color pop, but with Banana Escape's tropical supermarket chaos theme

## Style Pillars

1. Hero first
The banana must be instantly readable from a distance. Large face, strong silhouette, clean value contrast, clear highlight on peel.

2. Chunky obstacles
Obstacles should feel rounded, toy-like, and slightly exaggerated. Avoid thin detail and realistic textures.

3. Saturated but controlled
Use vivid yellows, oranges, mint greens, coral reds, aqua blues. Shadows should be warm-purple gray only as support, never muddy.

4. Mobile readability
Each obstacle, coin, and power-up must read in under half a second on a phone.

5. Motion-friendly shapes
Design forms that look good during fast downward movement and slight screen shake.

## World Theme

- Setting: tropical roadside escape near a playful market/smoothie district
- Tone: funny danger, not scary danger
- Story cue: a banana mascot is fleeing a ridiculous blender truck and supermarket hazards

## Main Character Direction

- Shape language: curved teardrop banana body, slightly tilted peel crown, oversized expressive eyes
- Personality: charming, fast, hopeful, a bit chaotic
- Surface: glossy peel with soft specular highlight
- Face: simple but premium, not babyish
- Limbs: tiny arms and shoes are okay, but keep them elegant and readable
- Animation attitude: squash, stretch, lean, lane-snap confidence

## Obstacle Direction

- Banana peel: slick, shiny hazard with curled tips
- Wooden crate: rounded market crate, warm wood, thick slats
- Cart: cute but dangerous grocery cart silhouette
- Rock: chunky smooth boulder with stylized edges
- Pit: comic broken asphalt hole with warm rim
- Smoothie box: bright fruit cargo crate with warning vibe

## Collectible Direction

- Coin: banana emblem coin with premium gold rim
- Combo banana: glowing special token, orange-gold energy
- Magnet: candy-finished horseshoe magnet, chunky and fun

## Background Direction

- Wide sunny sky
- Warm horizon glow
- Tropical palms and clean roadside shapes
- Road must stay neutral enough so yellow hero and gold coin pop clearly

## UI Direction

- Rounded cards, soft shadows, strong hierarchy
- White panels with fruit-color accents
- Big CTA buttons, friendly iconography
- Avoid tiny text and avoid gray-heavy UI

## Material Guide

- Hero: glossy peel, soft shadow, bright highlight
- Obstacles: semi-gloss toy finish
- Coins: metallic with strong highlight ring
- UI: frosted glass or creamy card surfaces

## Palette

- Banana yellow: `#FFD84D`
- Highlight yellow: `#FFF2A6`
- Peel edge gold: `#E3A92A`
- Leaf green: `#5CCB66`
- Mint accent: `#89F0C8`
- Orange accent: `#FF9854`
- Coral accent: `#FF6C5C`
- Sky blue: `#6CD7FF`
- Road charcoal: `#4B4550`
- Soft ink: `#3A3641`

## Camera / Composition Rules

- Keep hero centered in middle lane visually
- Obstacles should never visually merge into coins
- Reserve clean negative space around collectibles
- Important hazards need silhouette contrast against road

## Production Notes

- Prefer transparent PNG sprites for production art
- Keep consistent top-left lighting
- Favor 2D painted-cartoon assets over pseudo-3D realism
- Every asset should have a clean shadow pass or baked contact shadow

## Deliverables

- Character turnaround: idle, left lean, right lean, hit pose
- Obstacles: one polished sprite each
- Collectibles: coin, combo, magnet
- Blender truck: rear chase view + menu hero version
- UI icon set: score, meters, coins, sound, retry, pause, mission
